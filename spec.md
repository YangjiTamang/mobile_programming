Problem Statement

In Nepal, customers can order cakes and bakery items through general food-delivery platforms such as Pathao Food and Foodmandu, but ordering a highly customized cake can still require customers to contact bakeries separately through Instagram, Facebook, phone calls, or messaging apps. Although UG Cakes provides customization features and an AI assistant, it mainly serves its own brand and does not provide a marketplace where customers can communicate directly with multiple independent bakeries about their own reference designs. Customers may also face difficulties ensuring that delicate cakes are transported safely. Therefore, there is a need for a dedicated platform that simplifies custom cake inquiries, bakery communication, ordering, and safe delivery.

### Objectives

* To create a multi-bakery platform where customers can discover and compare different bakeries.
* To allow customers to upload cake designs and submit custom requirements such as flavor, size, decoration, message, budget and ingredient.
* To provide a direct chat system between customers and bakery owners/staff for discussing custom orders.
* To allow bakeries to respond to inquiries, provide customized quotations, and confirm orders.
* To provide specialized cake delivery using suitable packaging/carriers to reduce the risk of damage during transportation.
* To bring the customization, communication, ordering, payment, and delivery process into one platform.
